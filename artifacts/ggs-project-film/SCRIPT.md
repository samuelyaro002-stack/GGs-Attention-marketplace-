# GG’s Attention Marketplace — 30-second film

## Treatment
- **Format / runtime:** 16:9 landscape; exactly 30 seconds.
- **Promise:** Projects set the brief. Creators contribute. XP and allocations are visible; Daily G$ is a separate GoodDollar UBI feature.
- **Identity:** Existing GG logo; parchment #f8f4ed, ink #1d202f, coral #ff8259; Space Grotesk and DM Mono. Lime #b5df78 is the app’s supporting accent.
- **Hero:** The creator’s contribution becomes a legible ledger entry. A separate G$ disc distinguishes Daily UBI from campaign rewards.
- **Motion:** Masked type, paper assembly, large circular match cuts, ledger expansion. Crisp motions and shared geometry, not slide transitions.
- **Arc:** Immediate typographic hook; campaign-to-contribution assembly; measured transparency; bright Daily G$ reveal; account/wallet distinction; calm brand payoff.
- **Payoff:** A large official logo on parchment replaces all the feature artwork. No interactive CTA inside the composition.
- **Assets:** Existing marketplace logo.svg, copied without modification. Remaining artwork is procedural motion geometry and Lucide icons, not screenshots or live statistics.
- **Sound:** One warm instrumental electronic/funk bed, 108 BPM, gentle keys, rounded bass and crisp percussion. No narration or SFX. Intended musical accents are not claimed to be beat-synchronized.
